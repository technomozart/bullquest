Airdrop payout tool

Sends $BULL to every qualified wallet for a round. You sign every transaction in Phantom. This page never sees or asks for a private key, and it can never pay the same allocation twice.

1Sign in

This tool uses your founder session from the game. If the line below is red, open the app in this browser, sign in with your founder account, then come back and refresh.

Checking your session...

2Connect Phantom

The wallet you connect is the one that pays. It must hold the $BULL you are distributing plus a little SOL for fees (about 0.001 SOL per 6 wallets).

Not connected

3The token

Paste the $BULL mint address (the token's own address, from where you launched it). Then press Check token and make sure the supply and decimals look right before anything is sent.

4Rehearsal (do this once before the real run)

Send a tiny amount of the token to one wallet you control, for example your phone's wallet. If this works end to end, the big run uses exactly the same machinery.

5The round

Load a round to see exactly who gets what before anything is signed. Already-paid rows are skipped automatically, so if the browser closes mid-run you just come back, load the round again, and continue.

6Send

Wallets are paid in groups of 6 per transaction, and Phantom asks you to approve small batches of transactions at a time. Each allocation is locked in our database the moment its transaction is signed, before it is broadcast, so a mid-run crash can never cause a double payment.
