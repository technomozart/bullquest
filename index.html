How it works

Four steps, from your first bull to real stock

No wallet, no purchase, no crypto knowledge. The first three steps are live right now. The fourth is what we are building.

STEP 01

Choose your edition

Play now opens a choice. Classic is the original Solana run, still going for the players already there. The Robinhood Chain edition is the new one, and it is where the stock rewards live.

STEP 02

Duel it, earn GRIT

Send your bull into the Arena for fast one on one duels, run the daily quests, take the free spin and keep a login streak. All of it pays GRIT, and losing a duel never costs you any.

STEP 03

Train it at the Gym

Spend GRIT on Power, Speed and Stamina. This part is measured, not marketing: across 1,019,057 recorded duels an untrained bull wins 30.6% of the time and a well trained one wins about 75%.

STEP 04

Burn GRIT for real stock

Only 2,000 Genesis Bulls will ever exist, and they are not minted yet. Holding one puts you in the weekly pot: 70% of trading fees buy NVDA, TSLA, AAPL, AMZN and SPY, and burning GRIT during the week takes your share of it.

Three pillars

Three things a game token cannot say

Every play to earn economy dies the same way, because the reward is a coin the game printed itself. Here is what we changed.

The reward is real stock

A game token is only worth what the next buyer feels like paying. Tokenized equity is not. The weekly pot buys real stock on Robinhood Chain, so what you win holds its value whether or not anybody believes in BullQuest.

  • NVDA, TSLA, AAPL, AMZN and SPY, real ERC-20 tokens
  • 70% of trading fees fund the pot every week
  • Genesis Bulls mint on Robinhood Chain, not minted yet

Arena Duels

Fast head to head duels decided by your stats, your discipline and stance calls, your bull's rarity and level, and a bounded dose of luck. Training is the thing that moves the number, and it is the only thing you can grind.

  • You can never buy or edit raw stats
  • 30.6% win rate untrained, about 75% trained
  • A live leaderboard across 27,665 players

Sector Wars Live

Back one of eight market sectors with your bull, win duels for it, and split the pooled GRIT with the top backers when your sector takes the cycle. It unlocks on your second day, once your bull hits level 2. This one is live now.

  • Back a sector every 3-day cycle
  • Skill decides: win duels for your sector
  • Winners split the pooled GRIT

How rewards work

  1. Hold a Genesis BullRewards go to holders. Only 2,000 will ever exist, they live on Robinhood Chain, and the top of the leaderboard earns the first ones free. Everyone else mints or buys one. Not minted yet.
  2. Connect the wallet holding itConnect MetaMask so we can see your bull and know where to send your rewards. It is one signature: it costs nothing and moves nothing.
  3. Earn GRIT by playingSpins, quests and duel wins all add to your GRIT. Training at the Gym spends it, and playing earns it back.
  4. Burn GRIT on a stockDuring the week you burn GRIT on NVDA, TSLA, AAPL, AMZN or SPY. When the week closes the pot is split in proportion to how much GRIT each holder burned, capped per wallet so one holder cannot take the week.
  5. Paid to your walletNo claim window to miss and nothing to remember. Real tokenized stock lands in the wallet holding your Genesis Bull.

What we can and cannot promise

Rewards come from real trading fees, so the size of each week depends entirely on how much $BULL actually trades. We are not going to print a number we cannot stand behind. A quiet week is a small week, and we would rather say that now than dress it up. The exact fee share and the first payout date are announced before anything goes live.

Built to keep going

Rewards are paid out of trading fees, not out of a fixed slice of supply. That means nothing has to be printed to pay you, and the pool only exists when there is real activity behind it. A quiet week is a small week, and we would rather say so than promise a number we cannot stand behind.

$BULL is not live yet and no date has been announced. Your wallet key always stays with you. BullQuest will never ask for your private key or seed phrase.
